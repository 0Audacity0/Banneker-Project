Solar Eclipse Thingy

During a solar eclipse the moon passes between the sun and earth (because its a moon) and casts a TWO part shadow on earth's surface.

Umbra
The "dark" inner cone where the moon blocks all direct sunlight, meaning anybody who see inside it sees a total eclipse.
Penumbra
The "lighter" outer cone where the moon blocks only part of the sun. People who look inside it see a partial eclipse.

Note: I know this might be something stupid to say but the sun is about 400 times wider than the moon in real life, meaning the umbra reaches earth as a spot only about 100 through 270 km across.
